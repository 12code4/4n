# v2.0 "Forge & Fortune" — Implementation Notes

Theme: mastery over goods — craft what you delve. The company stops being a
pawnshop and becomes an industry.

## Systems

### Forge & Gear
- New building **Forge** (3 levels: unlock tiers I/II/III recipes).
- Gear slots per delver: **weapon / armor / trinket**.
  - weapon: `atk` added to strike/skill damage.
  - armor: `def` flat damage reduction (min 1 damage always).
  - trinket: one passive (`crit+`, `flee+`, `xp+`, `loot+`, `luck+`, `grit start+`).
- Crafting consumes materials + marks; recipes gated by forge level.
- Gear lives in `st.armory` (company pool); equip/unequip freely at home.
- Sellable at 60% of cost value.

### Alchemist (4th class)
- Base: vig 20, might 4, wits 8, luck 6. Hue: 95 (herbal green).
- Skill **Field Tonic** (2 grit): heal all living teammates `4 + wits*0.5`.
- Passive: bandages used by anyone heal +40% while an Alchemist stands.

### The Emberdeep (depths 4–6)
- Palette: slag red/deep umber, glow #ff5a2d.
- Materials: **Slag-Iron** (11), **Cinderbloom** (8), **Forge-Salt** (14),
  **King's Solder** (42, rare).
- Enemies: Ember Tick (swarm), Cinder Hound (beast/fire, fast), Slag Golem
  (construct, slow/heavy), Ashwake Chorister (undead/fire, buffs allies' damage),
  Bellows Wight (undead, aoe gusts), Molten Weaver (beast/fire, targets lowest).
- Guardian: **The Smelted King** — rotation: strike / crown of sparks (aoe) /
  reforge (heals self, first two times only).
- New hazards: Slag Flow (might), Ash Storm (wits), Heat Sink (luck).
- 6 new events (emberdeep-flavored) + journal pages 4–6.

### Contracts Board
- New building **Contracts Board** (L1 1 slot, L2 2 slots + better clients, L3 3).
- Offers refresh daily (0–2 new); each: deliver N× material by day D → payout
  well above market; forfeit fee if it expires.
- Fulfil from inventory with one click when holding enough.

### Market events & news ticker
- Daily 25% chance: a shock {material, mult 0.5–1.9, 3–5 days, headline}.
- Active shocks multiply the drifted price; expire silently, arrive loudly.
- Market tab shows the current headline; shocks also toast.

### Injuries
- Survivors who surface below 35% hp risk (50%) an injury: Cracked Ribs (−2 MGT),
  Torn Ligament (−2 WIT), Shaken Nerves (−2 LCK), Deep Gash (−6 max VIG).
- Heals after N days resting at home (infirmary level shortens).
- Injured delvers can still delve — at their reduced numbers.
