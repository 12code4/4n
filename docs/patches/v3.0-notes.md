# v3.0 "Rivals & Renown" — Implementation Notes

Theme: you are not the only one down there. The valley notices the company;
so does the competition.

## Systems

### Rival charters
- Two AI companies: **The Blacklantern Syndicate** (ruthless, fights dirty,
  poaches contracts) and **The Cartographers' Union** (methodical, races depth
  claims, trades fairly).
- Each has: renown score, deepest depth, a small simulated economy that advances
  on the day tick (they earn, they delve, they occasionally lose crews).
- Rivalry surfaces as:
  - **Contract sniping**: an open offer may vanish — "taken by the Syndicate".
  - **Depth claims**: first charter to slay a guardian gets a standing claim bonus
    (+5% sell prices biome-wide for the owner). Rivals CAN beat you to a guardian
    if you dawdle (pressure clock per biome, visible in Charter Hall).
  - **Underground encounters**: new map node type `rival` — parley (trade offer),
    wager (map intel for marks), or brawl (non-lethal; loser drops loot).
- The Standings panel (Charter Hall) shows all three companies' renown/depth.

### Renown & Charter Hall
- Renown earned: guardian kills (+major), contracts delivered, depth records,
  memorial honors. Lost: failed contracts, wipes.
- Ranks: Provisional → Chartered → Established → Honored → The Gilded (5 tiers);
  each tier: a perk (hire discount, +1 contract slot, peddler stock discount,
  +5% sell, tavern draws a 4th class veteran…).
- **Charter Hall** building: unlocks the standings, rank perks, and rival intel.

### The Sunken Archive (depths 7–9)
- Drowned library civilisation; teal-ink palette; slow water light.
- Materials: **Ink-Pearl** (16), **Vellum of the Deep** (13), **Custodian Brass**
  (18), **A Complete Sentence** (50, rare — the Archive's true currency).
- Enemies: Page Swarm (swarm), Ink Revenant (undead, 'tithe'-like steals loot),
  Custodian (construct, guards: takes hits for allies), Drowned Scholar (caster,
  'chorus'-like: curses — pre-status-effect: lowers party damage), Lantern Eel
  (beast, fast crit), Misfiled Thing (???, random behavior).
- Guardian: **The Librarian** — rotation includes 'silence' (grit drain) and
  'index' (marks the delver who dealt most damage; next hit on them is doubled).

### Delver skill choices
- At levels 3, 6, 9: choose 1 of 2 kit upgrades per class (e.g. Vanguard 3:
  "Wider Shield" guard covers adjacent ally / "Iron Taunt" taunt +1 round).
- Stored as `d.talents: [ids]`; combat consults `G.Delvers.talent(d, id)`.

### Company relics
- Guardian first-kills and rare events award **relics** (company-level equips,
  max 2 slotted): e.g. *The Warden's Bell* (start fights with +1 Grit, enemies
  +1 spd), *A Crown, Cooling* (+10% sell prices, hire costs +20%), *The First
  Rope* (flee always succeeds, drop 50% loot).
- Slotted in Charter Hall; each has an upside and a cost.

### Memorial & achievements
- Memorial wall in town scene grows a plank per grave; graveyard panel lists all.
- "Honor the fallen" once per death: small marks cost → renown + roster morale
  (temp +1 luck next expedition).
- ~20 achievements (first guardian, 1000ᵯ day, no-torch run, full-clear floor…)
  in a Ledger tab.

### Townsfolk questlines (3-step each)
- **Petra**: the coin mystery — deliver pale coins → assay experiment → reveal:
  the coins are minted BELOW; unlocks 'coin' market intel perk.
- **Dov**: Maren's tab — pay it (large) or delve her favorite route; unlocks
  tavern 4th-slot veteran pool.
- **The Silent Priest**: three specific relics traded for bone bells; rings once
  per biome; unlocks graveyard blessing (dead delvers' XP partly returns as
  company renown).
