# v5.0 "The Heart of It" — Implementation Notes

Theme: the last trade. Everything the game has taught converges at depth 13,
and the whole surface gets its final coat of lacquer.

## The finale

### Depth 13 — The Heart
- Entry gated on the Auricle (depth 12 guardian). The map is HAND-AUTHORED:
  a single narrowing path — Antechamber (rest) → three Trials (fight/event
  hybrids that quote earlier biomes) → **The Heart of the Maw**.
- The Heart encounter is part boss, part negotiation, in phases:
  1. It fights (hardest fight in the game; uses mood + intent + status systems).
  2. At half health it STOPS — and opens the Ledger: it shows the company's
     lifetime numbers (real stats: taken vs given, delvers spent, honors paid)
     and asks the question from Maren's note in reverse: *what do you want?*
  3. **Three endings**:
     - **Seal it** — collapse the stair. The valley is safe, poor, and quiet.
       (Company becomes a memorial trust; epilogue over the town at dawn.)
     - **Trade with it** — sign the charter it offers. Perpetual partnership:
       endless-mode economy with the Maw as licensed supplier (post-game
       continues, prices stabilize, a 14th "depth" appears: the Vault).
     - **Become it** — Maren's choice, finished. The Heart takes a new keeper;
       the player character descends; the epilogue plays as the MAW's ledger,
       and New-Charter+ starts with the sign repainted: "MAW & CO."
- Ending epilogues: canvas-painted scenes + text; choice recorded in the codex;
  New-Charter+ (any ending) = prestige with a bonus Legacy tier.

### Daily Descent
- Seeded from the UTC date: fixed founding roster, fixed omens, fixed mood
  calendar, 10 in-game days, score = marks + relic value + depth record.
- Local best-score board (per seed); seed string shareable/enterable.

## The soundscape (WebAudio, no files)
- Engine: tiny synth module (osc + noise + biquad + delay), master mute toggle,
  volume persisted.
- Biome drones: Gullet (low sine + glass shimmer), Emberdeep (brown noise
  furnace + crackle), Archive (watery chorus), Veins (heartbeat at the map's
  pulse rate), Heart (the motif, held).
- SFX: UI ticks, coin counts, forge ring, combat impacts/crits, death knell,
  journal music-box motif (Maren's theme, 9 notes).

## The last polish pass
- **Title screen**: painted vista of Hollowbrook over the rift; Continue/New/Daily.
- **Codex**: every enemy, material, relic, character, mood discovered → entry
  with the flavor text collected in one place.
- **Stats ledger**: lifetime numbers page (the same numbers the Heart reads).
- Lighting layer: warm pools around lit windows/torches; vignette; wipes
  between screens; parallax dust everywhere.
- Onboarding: contextual first-time hints (first hire, first descent, first
  death, first contract), each shown once, stored in flags.
- Balance overhaul from a fresh round of simulated campaigns across all depths.
